# Review questions for NeuroMPS

least able to check ourselves, in the order we would most value a second opinion. Section numbers
refer to that report.

1. §18.1 — the absence of a dose signal in the features. Does that match your experience of
   bicuculline series on MEAs, or does it suggest our feature definitions are wrong?
2. §12 — mean firing rate reversing sign for the same mechanism across two platforms. Plausible, or
   a sign we have mis-paired something?
3. §10 — that chamber-level readouts do not recover axonal direction. Is anyone relying on this
   inference in practice?
4. §14 — the electrode-overlap argument. Does the spacing-versus-catchment problem bite in real
   arrays the way we claim it does in simulated ones?
5. §3 — whether a twofold disagreement in "bursts per minute" between two implementations matches
   what you see when you change analysis software.
