Generated by `neuromps.models.feature_definitions` from `outputs/metrics.json` key `feature_definitions.translation`. Ratio is the target set's value over ours (neuromps) on the same recording; rho is Spearman; within 10% is the share of recordings whose ratio is within 10% of 1. Verdict: same number (ratio within 0.05 of 1, rho >= 0.95), scale factor (rho >= 0.95, ratio outside), no conversion (rho < 0.95). The last two columns say how close the target set itself is to the depositors' published values: a same-number verdict between two of our sets does not mean either matches the depositors. The per-plate column is the range of the median ratio over the three plates; a factor that differs by plate is a property of the cultures, not of the definitions. kapucu2022_logisi is experimental and supports no claim. Checked only on these plates; not a conversion for other labs.
