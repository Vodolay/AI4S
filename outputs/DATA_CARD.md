## Limitations

- Spike detection is the publishers', not ours, in every source.
- Records are comparable only within a data level (see `schema/STANDARD.md` section 3).
- Electrode counts differ by orders of magnitude across platforms; electrode-dependent features must not be compared without explicit rescaling.
- QC flags are informational: a flagged record is not automatically excluded.
